# Build your path

An eight-week project plan you can adapt to your starting point. It is a suggested schedule, not a promise of job readiness.

## Weeks 1–2 · Choose a real problem

Read three relevant employer job descriptions. Record responsibilities, required skills, seniority, location, and customer exposure. Pick one role family and a small user problem. Set a success metric and a baseline before writing the solution.

## Weeks 3–4 · Build a working slice

Use Python or TypeScript to deliver one end-to-end workflow. Add version control, reproducible setup, sensible access controls, and clear error handling. Keep the scope small enough to finish.

## Weeks 5–6 · Measure and improve

Create a test set that reflects the intended use. Track failures, latency, and cost. Check what happens when inputs are missing, hostile, or outside the system’s scope. Compare against your baseline and document uncertainty.

## Weeks 7–8 · Publish your evidence

Write a case study: problem, users, architecture, alternatives, results, failures, and next steps. Include a runnable demo or recorded walkthrough. Use synthetic or properly authorized data. Ask a peer to follow your setup instructions.

## Application checklist

- Link to one relevant project and explain your contribution.
- Match your examples to the job’s responsibilities.
- Describe measured outcomes without inflated claims.
- Prepare a tradeoff story and a debugging story.
- Ask about the team’s actual work and expectations.
