# Contribute to the atlas

Help make AI career research clearer and more useful.

1. Open an issue with an employer source and the exact title you want to add.
2. Explain which responsibilities make the role distinct.
3. Separate facts in the posting from your interpretation.
4. Add an access date and describe any geographic or seniority limits.
5. Suggest a small portfolio project that demonstrates the work.

Avoid unsupported salary claims, job guarantees, and lists of tools without explaining what people do with them.

[Open the GitHub repository](https://github.com/vvm3-IT/ai-career-atlas)
