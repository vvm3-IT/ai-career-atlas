# Inference / ML Systems Engineer

> Infrastructure · Role field guide · Reviewed November 5, 2026

## The work

Make model serving faster, more reliable, and efficient.

## Typical responsibilities

The following is an editorial role profile, not a quoted vacancy or a universal job description.

- Define the problem and the evidence needed to judge success.
- Build and iterate on the systems or experiments within the role’s focus.
- Investigate failures and communicate tradeoffs to collaborators.
- Document decisions, limitations, and operational ownership.

## Skills to demonstrate

Systems programming, profiling, distributed systems, GPU fundamentals, and performance measurement.

## Portfolio project

Benchmark a model-serving workload across batch sizes and concurrency, reporting throughput, tail latency, and hardware.

Include a reproducible setup, a baseline, an evaluation, and a short write-up of what failed. Explain your individual contribution.

## Questions to ask an employer

Ask about hardware, on-call expectations, and whether work centers on kernels, serving, or platform operations.

- What does success look like in the first 90 days?
- How much time is spent coding, meeting customers, or running experiments?
- What experience is required versus teachable on the job?
- Who owns quality, security, and production incidents?
