# Explore AI roles

Choose by the kind of work you enjoy, then inspect actual job descriptions. These are overlapping role families, not a standardized industry taxonomy.

## [Forward Deployed Engineer](/roles/forward-deployed-engineer)

**Customer delivery.** Turn an ambiguous customer problem into a working AI system.

## [AI / Applied AI Engineer](/roles/ai-engineer)

**Product engineering.** Build useful software around models, retrieval, tools, and evaluation.

## [Applied AI Architect](/roles/applied-ai-architect)

**Technical advisory.** Design how AI fits into a customer’s systems and business workflows.

## [AI Evaluation Engineer](/roles/evaluation-engineer)

**Quality & measurement.** Measure model and system behavior with repeatable tests and useful failure categories.

## [Research Engineer](/roles/research-engineer)

**Research & experimentation.** Translate research questions into experiments, infrastructure, and reproducible results.

## [Inference / ML Systems Engineer](/roles/inference-engineer)

**Infrastructure.** Make model serving faster, more reliable, and efficient.

## [AI Security / Safeguards Engineer](/roles/ai-security-engineer)

**Security & reliability.** Identify and reduce security risks in AI applications and model behavior.
