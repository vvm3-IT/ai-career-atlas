## What we observed

Employer careers pages show distinct titles across customer delivery, applied engineering, research, evaluations, performance, and safeguards. The directory groups related titles into navigable role families. “AI forward engineer” is interpreted here as **forward deployed engineer**; employers may use other wording.

## What we infer

Role descriptions, skills, comparison advice, and project suggestions are editorial synthesis. They are not copied employer job descriptions. AI evaluation and AI security are broad categories that can appear under several titles. This atlas does not claim that all listed careers are newly invented or that hiring demand is rising at a measured rate.

## Keep the research useful

For each future job-description analysis, record the employer, exact title, source URL, access date, location, seniority, responsibilities, required and preferred skills, and any stated compensation with currency and location. Separate direct observations from interpretation. Never infer a requirement that the posting does not state.

Revisit sources before applying. Expired vacancies should be labeled as historical evidence, not presented as active openings. Salary comparisons require comparable seniority, location, and compensation definitions; no salary estimates are included yet.
